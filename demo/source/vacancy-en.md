Vacancy: AI Engineer (32–40 hours)
Organisation: City of Riverton, Data & Innovation team (fictional organisation)

About the role
The City of Riverton wants to help residents faster and better with AI. Together with policy
advisers and customer-service staff you build applications based on language models, from
prototype to production. Think of an assistant that helps staff answer residents' questions with
the right regulations at hand, and automatic summaries of formal objections. Reliability, privacy
and explainability come first.

What you will do
- Design and build AI applications with language models (RAG, agents) in Python.
- Take applications to production and run them, together with the platform team.
- Measure answer quality and build guardrails against hallucinations.
- Translate questions from policy and operations into feasible solutions and explain choices to
  non-technical colleagues.

Requirements
- A completed degree at bachelor level or higher in computer science, AI, data science or similar.
- At least 3 years of experience with Python in production environments.
- Demonstrable experience with applications based on language models (for example RAG).
- Experience with CI/CD, containers (Docker) and version control (Git).
- Good written and spoken English.

Nice to have
- Experience evaluating LLM output and building guardrails.
- Knowledge of privacy law (GDPR) and working with personal data.
- Experience with open-source or self-hosted language models.
- Experience in the public sector or with complex stakeholders.

What we offer
A salary up to EUR 6,110 gross per month (36 hours), a 17% flexible benefits budget, hybrid
working and room for training.
